What makes a Green Ambassador?

No perfect résumé required. We look for reliability, initiative and a genuine willingness to contribute.

  • ✓Flexible participation built around school, university or work schedules.
  • ✓Opportunities to build leadership, organising and communication experience.
  • ✓Support for photography, videography, design, event planning and other useful skills.
  • ✓A growing network of youth from different parts of Malaysia.

FAQ

Quick answers.

A few things people usually want to know before joining or collaborating.

What is Youth Action Collective?

YAC is a youth-led Malaysian initiative that connects young people with environmental and community action, supports youth volunteering and helps local action grow through a shared national network.

What is a Green Ambassador?

Green Ambassadors are a different name for Volunteers in Youth Action Collective, They can take part in flexible service activities, complete YAC challenges, document their impact and can contribute skills such as planning, design, outreach, photography or videography.

Do I need volunteering experience?

No. Reliability, willingness to learn and genuine interest in contributing matter more than having an impressive background.

Can organisations work with YAC?

Yes. YAC is open to discussing volunteer support, community projects, school or university outreach, environmental action and other suitable collaborations.
